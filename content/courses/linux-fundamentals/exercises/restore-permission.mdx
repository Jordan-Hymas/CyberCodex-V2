## Learn

Permissions describe read, write, and execute rights. u+r adds the owner read bit without replacing other bits. The teaching shell acts as the owner of challenge files.

Permission bits are grouped for owner, group, and others. r means read, w means write, and x means execute or traverse. This simulator models access as the owner; it does not provide multi-user operating-system permissions. Symbolic changes such as u+r are useful because they preserve unrelated bits.

## Syntax example

```sh
stat sample.txt
chmod u+r sample.txt
```

Example filenames illustrate syntax; inspect your mission files before running them.

## Your mission

Inspect sealed.txt, restore owner read permission, and read it.

## Common mistake

File readability and parent-directory traversal are separate requirements.
