## For operators, not tourists

This is the flagship. The first chapters sharpen professional habits: input redirection, `tee`, `diff`, variables, directory traversal, conditionals, `wc`, `tr`, numeric sorting and safe cleanup.

Then comes the **Operator tier**, modelled on how real hacking-practice platforms work. The flag lives in `/flag`, owned by `root`, and you cannot read it. Each mission installs a program that runs with root's power and only hands the flag over when you launch it correctly: by the right path, from the right directory, with the right arguments, streams or environment variables. Seven chapters, 29 missions, decoys designed to punish guessing.

This course is **fully locked** to CyberCodex Elite. Finish **Linux Fundamentals** and **Intermediate Linux** first.

## Your own private Linux

Sign in and each mission gives you a personal, saved Linux environment. Commands change your own files, never anyone else's. Reset restores the mission and rolls a fresh flag; completed progress is kept. The left side of every mission is a full written lesson with worked examples; the right side is your terminal.

## What the terminal supports

This is a focused teaching simulation, not a full virtual machine. Type `help` for the exact command set. Full Bash scripting, real process control, package installs, networking and kernel behaviour live in later, separately isolated labs.
